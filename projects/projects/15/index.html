Rose Water

Product Visual Design & Branding Poster

Project Overview

This project focuses on creating a professional product advertisement poster inspired by real-world cosmetic branding. The goal was to visually communicate freshness, softness, and skincare benefits while maintaining strong brand identity and visual clarity.

Project Description

The poster was designed using a soft pastel color palette, floral elements, and water splash effects to reflect the product’s rose-based formula and cleansing properties. Careful attention was given to composition, lighting, shadows, and typography to ensure the product remains the central focus. The layout follows advertising design principles, balancing aesthetics with clear product visibility and call-to-action placement.

“Good product design makes you feel the quality before you ever use it.”

Learning Outcomes & Purpose
The main goal of this project was to strengthen my skills in visual branding and product-focused graphic design. Through this project, I gained practical experience in:

⦿ Creating professional marketing visuals for cosmetic products
⦿ Using composition, color harmony, and lighting to enhance product appeal
⦿ Applying typography and layout principles for advertising design
⦿ Designing visuals that align with real brand identities and market standards
