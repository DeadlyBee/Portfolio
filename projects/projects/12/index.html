Project Description

Valantino is a 3D modeling and rendering project created in Blender, focused on designing a realistic perfume bottle with attention to form, materials, and lighting. The project demonstrates my ability to model clean surfaces, apply realistic shaders, and produce high-quality renders suitable for product visualization.

Materials and shaders were carefully adjusted to create realistic reflections and surface details, while the lighting setup was optimized to highlight the bottle’s shape and enhance visual depth. Camera positioning and rendering settings were used to produce a polished final presentation.

This project reflects my growing skills in 3D modeling, scene composition, and visual storytelling through digital assets.

“A focus on form, light, and material to create a refined 3D product visualization.”

Objectives & Skills Demonstrated

⦿ 3D modeling with clean and optimized geometry
⦿ Managing and displaying dynamic data
⦿ Material and shader creation for realistic surfaces
⦿ Lighting setup for product visualization
⦿ Rendering high-quality still images
