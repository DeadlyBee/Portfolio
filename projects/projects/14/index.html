BOGA

Conceptual Beverage Advertising Design

Project Overview

This project focuses on creating a bold and refreshing advertisement poster for BOGA, inspired by carbonated lemon beverages. The visual concept highlights the drink’s freshness by deconstructing the bottle into layered slices combined with water splash and natural elements.

Project Description

The design features an exploded bottle effect where the drink is visually separated into sections, revealing lemon slices inside to symbolize flavor intensity and freshness. Mint leaves and water splash effects were added to enhance motion and realism. A monochromatic green palette was chosen to reinforce the lemon identity while maintaining visual harmony and strong product focus. Lighting, shadows, and depth were carefully adjusted to create a vibrant and energetic composition.

“A refreshing design should feel cold, fresh, and alive — even on a still image.”

Learning Outcomes & Purpose
The main goal of this project was to explore creative advertising concepts and advanced photo manipulation techniques. Through this project, I gained practical experience in:

⦿ Creating dynamic compositions using exploded-view effects
⦿ Blending multiple visual elements into a cohesive scene
⦿ Enhancing realism with shadows, lighting, and splash effects
⦿ Designing eye-catching beverage advertisements aligned with brand identity
