Valantino 0.2

3D Product Animation & VFX (Blender)

Project Overview

Valantino 0.2 is an advanced 3D animation and visual effects project developed in Blender, building upon the original Valantino perfume model. This version focuses on motion, cinematic presentation, and visual storytelling through animation and VFX, transforming a static product into a dynamic visual scene.

Project Description

TIn this project, the original Valantino perfume bottle was reused and enhanced through animated sequences and visual effects. The goal was to elevate the product presentation by adding movement, transitions, lighting effects, and cinematic camera work.

The scene was designed to emphasize elegance and luxury, using controlled lighting, smooth animations, and VFX elements to create visual impact. Special attention was given to timing, camera paths, and compositing to ensure a polished and engaging final result.

Valantino 0.2 demonstrates my ability to move beyond static 3D modeling and into animated product visualization, combining design, motion, and atmosphere into a cohesive visual narrative.

“Turning a static product into a cinematic experience through motion, light, and visual effects.”

Learning Outcomes & Purpose
The main goal of this project was to deepen my understanding of how visual production and animation techniques can be used to enhance digital content. Through this project, I gained practical experience in:

⦿ Creating and animating 3D assets with a focus on visual storytelling
⦿ Applying lighting, camera movement, and cinematic composition
⦿ TApplying lighting, camera movement, and cinematic composition
⦿ Managing a full creative workflow from concept to final render
